# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual learners preparing for certification exams. They bring their own notes, PDFs, course material, or study documents and want to convert that material into focused practice without manually writing questions.

Their context is practical and time-sensitive: they are studying alone, checking readiness, and using the app to turn source material into realistic review sessions.

## Product Purpose

This product turns learner-provided study material into grounded certification-style practice exams. Success means a learner can add trusted source material, select what to study, generate a useful exam set, and review answers with enough explanation and evidence to understand gaps.

The product should reduce setup friction, make background processing understandable, and keep the user oriented through a clear Sources, Generate, Review, and History workflow.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, elegant, smooth.

The interface should feel precise and composed rather than playful or noisy. It should give users confidence that their material is being handled carefully and that generated questions are tied back to the source.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy LMS dashboards, enterprise admin panels, cluttered upload portals, and generic AI SaaS layouts with excessive gradients, glass effects, or repeated identical cards.

Dribbble and Behance are useful references for polish, spacing, motion, and visual craft, but the app should not become a decorative concept shot. The workflow must stay clear and usable.

## Design Principles

- Source material is the anchor: always show what the user uploaded, what is ready, and what will be used for generation.
- Calm progress beats technical noise: explain background work in plain language while keeping technical details available on demand.
- Practice should feel interactive: questions should invite action through choices, hints, answer reveal, explanation, and evidence.
- Trust is earned through transparency: show statuses, errors, selected sources, and generated evidence without making users inspect raw payloads.
- Reduce decision load: guide the user through one primary path at a time, then let advanced options stay adjustable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. The app should be keyboard usable, readable at common zoom levels, and friendly to reduced motion preferences.

Use clear focus states, sufficient color contrast, non-color-only status indicators, concise labels, and predictable navigation between Sources, Generate, Review, and History.
